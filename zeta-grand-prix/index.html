    How it’s drawn

    The circuit and the island are real 3D geometry. Realistic renders at full screen resolution with soft shadows, glossy car paint and glass towers whose windows light up at dusk and night. Pixel art renders it small (the pixel size slider sets how small), outlines it wherever depth jumps, then snaps it to one fixed palette of 90 colours. The island is a grid of voxel columns, terraced at the rim and banded in sand and sandstone below. Nothing is a sprite. Grandstands and buildings between the camera and the action rise out of the way as you drive.

    Teams and drivers are invented, and the venue is inspired by Dubai rather than a map of it. Your car is a real arcade physics model: grip comes from the tyre, the track surface and downforce, so you can understeer, slide, run wide onto the grass or into the gravel and hit the barriers. The other cars run the circuit on a computed racing line with braking points, overtakes and pit stops of their own.
